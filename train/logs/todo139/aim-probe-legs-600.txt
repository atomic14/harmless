by shot, a warhead is 250 points (2638 landed across the run), and contact is what
is left. the aim columns above describe the FIRST of the three only — which
is the point: read them beside the split, not instead of it.

a face down, destroyed and they lost are train/survivability.ts's own
outcome columns, watched its way — every step, because a face that was
flattened and came back is still a face that was flattened. ENERGY LOW is
the console's own line (systems.ts `energyLow`): the last bank, where the
shield stops recovering at all and a player is meant to break off. it is
the term docs/TODO/139 M2 states its gate in, and today it is unreachable.
