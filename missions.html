THE MISSION TOUR

Five patrons across galaxy one

Bound with the Space Trader's Flight Training Manual · the missions as the game offers them

The original Elite gave you one mission, from the Navy, once you had sixteen kills. HARMLESS keeps that one and adds a tour. Five patrons wait on five worlds, each four to six jumps farther across galaxy one than the last. Each has a face, a name and a way of speaking, and each offers an arc of two to four legs. Finish an arc, or fail it, and the next patron sends word. You hear it as a rumour on the station board a few jumps out, and as a message when you dock one jump away.

Nothing here spoils an ending. This page shows what a patron tells you before you accept, and no more. Your own log is in the game, on ⇧R at any station.

The patrons' names and words were written by a language model from the 1984 seeds, checked by the game's own rules, and committed. Elite (1984) is by David Braben and Ian Bell; this is an unaffiliated fan remake.
